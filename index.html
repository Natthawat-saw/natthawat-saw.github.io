Section A

Profile

I'm a chemical engineer by training and a quality engineer by trade. My work starts with a real problem on the line: a complaint that keeps coming back, a report that three people type by hand, a paper form that gets printed, scanned and thrown away.

At Union Nifco, an automotive parts maker, I handle customer complaints with OEM customers using 8D, Why-Why and Fishbone analysis, and I run the daily morning meeting where shop-floor issues reach management. That job taught me where data breaks down in a factory. So I started fixing it: an HTML dashboard for QC complaints, a Flask app that turns LINE chat exports into the morning-meeting Excel, and Power Automate flows that route parts and alerts without anyone retyping them.

At NIDA I took the same habit into AI. My independent study, TISI-RAG, makes more than a thousand scanned Thai Industrial Standards searchable with OCR, hybrid retrieval and an LLM that cites its sources. I graduate with honors in December 2026.

I'm looking for a Data Scientist, AI Engineer or Data Analyst role where domain knowledge counts. I check my outputs against real data before anyone else sees them, and I'll say so when a model doesn't work.

REC-13 · 2026Experiment

Gold price signals: a negative result I kept

Personal research with MetaTrader 5

I tested whether daily and 5-minute models could trade gold. I used strict no-lookahead backtests, so each prediction only saw data from before it. The daily model hit about 50% direction accuracy, and every mean-reversion setup lost money after spread. I stopped instead of tuning until the backtest looked good.
